# Source: ICSC — new grocery formats, growth, and investment

Source: `raw/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md` (ICSC Exchange article,
retrieved 2026-09-11)

## Summary

Industry-wide trade article on how the ~$912B U.S. grocery sector is
stratifying: specialty and value grocers are gaining share while
traditional supermarkets pull back, national chains are investing heavily
in remodels and small formats, and grocery-anchored real estate investment
surged in 2025. See [[concepts/grocery-industry-trends]] for the synthesis
and why it matters for this engagement.

## Key facts

- U.S. grocery sector: ~$912.4B annually; ~40,000 grocery-anchored retail
  centers nationwide (JLL STARS database).
- Trader Joe's (a specialty grocer) grew 3% YoY, outperforming the broader
  sector by 6 points, with double-digit YoY growth among Gen Z shoppers —
  while traditional grocers like Publix and Safeway saw pullbacks.
- Target's food-and-beverage segment: $24B+ in fiscal 2025 sales;
  repositioning around "emerging brands, wellness and owned brands" rather
  than competing as an "everything grocer."
- Walmart: remodeling 650+ stores in 2026, ~20 new stores in 2026-2027,
  $5.5B allocated for fiscal 2026 remodels.
- Costco: targeting 30+ new stores/year, 28 net openings expected fiscal
  2026, ~640 U.S. locations.
- Aldi: testing a modular store format (launched Aventura, FL, Sept. 2025)
  for smaller urban footprints; opening 150 U.S. stores this year.
- Grocery-anchored center transactions: ~$11B in 2025, up 42% from 2024
  (Brixton Capital, Medipower, Nuveen, and Blackstone deals cited).
- Gelson's "ReCharge" micromarkets: 3,000-5,400 sq. ft. format pairing
  groceries with EV charging, lounges, and fast-casual food (Southern
  California).
- Tillster 2026 survey: 36% of consumers visit grocery stores more often,
  33% shop convenience stores more often, and 29-37% reduce fast-food/
  fast-casual visits — grocery and c-stores are capturing dining
  occasions from restaurants.

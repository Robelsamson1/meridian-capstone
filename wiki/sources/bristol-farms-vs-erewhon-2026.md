## Summary

Local LA trade-press piece on [[entities/bristol-farms]], a 13-store
(soon 14) Southern California specialty grocer, expanding directly in Los
Angeles and positioning itself as a lower-priced, utility-focused
alternative to [[entities/erewhon]]'s premium/celebrity-driven brand. This
is the first source in this wiki with a concrete, store-level example of
specialty-grocer expansion inside Meridian's own metro area, rather than
national-level trend data. See [[concepts/store-expansion-strategy]] and
[[concepts/prepared-foods-positioning]] for why it matters.

## Key facts

- Bristol Farms: founded 1982 (Rolling Hills Estates), 13 locations
  currently, 14th opening in Hollywood mid-April 2026. Parent company:
  Good Food Holdings (also owns Lazy Acres).
- Positioned explicitly as the "practical" / "everyman's" alternative to
  Erewhon — prepared foods, sushi counters, smoked meats, bakery, floral,
  in-house scratch kitchens and seasonal juice programs, without
  Erewhon-level pricing or celebrity branding.
- Example pricing: a signature "Cookie" item at $4; smoothies/bottled
  juices starting ~$9 (cited as notably cheaper than Erewhon's comparable
  items).
- Targets both affluent suburbs (Newport Beach, Palm Desert) and Los
  Angeles neighborhoods directly with new openings.
- Named leadership: Steve Howard (SVP Retail — "We're the Disneyland of
  food"), Dan Evon (VP Culinary), Joyce de Brevannes (VP Marketing).

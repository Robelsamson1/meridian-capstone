# Store expansion strategy

Source: [[sources/client-brief]]

## History

[[entities/meridian-markets]] grew from 6 stores to 14 in five years,
primarily by taking over leases from chains that exited neighborhoods
Meridian considered underserved.

## Current decision

Leadership sees a Pasadena site as "the obvious next step" but wants
sales/category data to confirm the decision before committing. This is the
direct motivation for the requested dashboard.

## Complication

Growth hasn't been even — some of the 14 stores took off immediately,
others have been slower to find their footing. Understanding what
separates the two groups is likely central to both the dashboard and the
Pasadena decision.

## Relevant data

Store attributes (square footage, opening date, lease terms) are
classified **Open** for AI-tool use per the client brief, unlike most other
data types. See `docs/data-handling-checklist.md`.

## Industry context

Grocery-anchored real estate investment surged in 2025 (~$11B in deals, up
42% YoY), and national/regional players (Aldi, Gelson's) are actively
building smaller-footprint formats similar to Meridian's own approach.
Grocery-anchored centers nationally also run tighter (4.0% vacancy, a 4.4%
rent premium) than non-anchored retail, and new-store growth in 2025 was
heavily concentrated in the Southeast and Mid-Atlantic — outside
Meridian's LA/Orange/Ventura footprint, so the national growth story isn't
a direct read on the Pasadena site specifically. See
[[concepts/grocery-industry-trends]].

## Local competitive landscape

[[entities/bristol-farms]], a similarly-sized Southern California
specialty grocer, is actively opening new Los Angeles stores (14th
location, Hollywood, mid-April 2026), competing on the same prepared-foods/
scratch-kitchen/small-format terms as Meridian. This is the first
store-level (rather than national-trend) evidence of specialty-grocer
expansion inside Meridian's own metro area — a useful comparable for how
Pasadena might be evaluated, though it isn't confirmed to overlap with
Meridian's own trade areas. See [[analyses/open-questions]].

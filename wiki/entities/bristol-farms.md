# Bristol Farms

Source: [[sources/bristol-farms-vs-erewhon-2026]]

## Overview

Southern California specialty grocery chain, founded 1982 in Rolling Hills
Estates. Operates 13 stores, with a 14th opening in Hollywood mid-April
2026. Parent company is Good Food Holdings, which also owns Lazy Acres.

## Positioning

Competes on prepared foods, sushi counters, smoked meats, bakery, and
floral — emphasizing utility and breadth rather than scarcity or
exclusivity. Explicitly framed in coverage as the lower-priced, practical
alternative to [[entities/erewhon]]'s premium/celebrity-driven brand,
without a comparable price markup.

## Relevance to Meridian

A specialty grocer of a similar size/format to [[entities/meridian-markets]]
that is actively opening new Los Angeles stores and competing on very
similar terms (prepared foods, local/scratch kitchens, smaller-format
utility). Not confirmed to compete directly with Meridian in any specific
neighborhood, but a relevant comparable for the Pasadena expansion
decision. See [[concepts/store-expansion-strategy]] and
[[analyses/open-questions]].

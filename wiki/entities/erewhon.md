# Erewhon

Source: [[sources/bristol-farms-vs-erewhon-2026]]

## Overview

Los Angeles specialty grocery chain known for premium pricing and
celebrity-driven branding. Referenced in this wiki only as the competitive
foil in coverage of [[entities/bristol-farms]]'s expansion — no source yet
ingested is about Erewhon directly.

## Relevance to Meridian

Cited as the premium end of the LA specialty-grocer market that
[[entities/bristol-farms]] is positioning against with lower prices and a
broader, less exclusivity-driven offering. Useful as a reference point for
where [[entities/meridian-markets]]'s own prepared-foods/pricing
positioning sits in the LA specialty-grocer landscape. See
[[concepts/prepared-foods-positioning]] and [[analyses/open-questions]].

## Company Overview

Bristol Farms is a Southern California grocery chain founded in 1982 in
Rolling Hills Estates by Irv Gronsky and Mike Burbank. It currently
operates 13 locations, with a 14th store opening in Hollywood in mid-April
2026. Parent company is Good Food Holdings, which also owns Lazy Acres.

## Positioning

The article frames Bristol Farms as "the practical Angeleno's answer to
Erewhon" — offering restaurant-quality prepared foods, sushi counters,
smoked meats, bakery, and floral arrangements, with utility and breadth
emphasized over scarcity or exclusivity. Where Erewhon emphasizes celebrity
partnerships and premium branding, Bristol Farms offers chef-led food and
ingredients without a comparable price markup — described as the
"everyman's Erewhon."

Steve Howard (SVP of Retail): "We're the Disneyland of food."

## Product & Pricing Examples

- "The Cookie" — a 6-ounce warm chocolate item, $4.
- Smoothies/bottled juices start around $9, notably cheaper than
  Erewhon's comparable offerings.
- In-house scratch kitchens and seasonal juice programs.

## Market Strategy

Bristol Farms targets both affluent suburbs (Newport Beach, Palm Desert)
and Los Angeles neighborhoods directly. Named leadership: Dan Evon (VP
Culinary), Joyce de Brevannes (VP Marketing), in addition to Steve Howard
(SVP Retail).

# New grocery formats, store growth, and investment (ICSC)

## Market Overview

The U.S. grocery sector generates approximately $912.4 billion annually.
JLL tracks around 40,000 grocery-anchored retail centers nationwide
through its STARS database.

## Format and Strategy Shifts

**Specialty Grocers Gaining Share**: Trader Joe's achieved 3%
year-over-year growth (period ending Feb. 28), outperforming the broader
grocery sector by 6 percentage points. Consumer Edge noted the brand shows
"double-digit year-over-year growth among Gen Z shoppers," while
traditional grocers like Publix and Safeway experience pullbacks across
income groups.

**Target's Repositioning**: Target's food-and-beverage segment generated
over $24 billion in fiscal 2025 sales. The company is pursuing a
"distinctive grocery destination" strategy emphasizing "emerging brands,
wellness and owned brands" rather than competing as an "everything
grocer."

**Walmart Expansion**: Walmart is remodeling over 650 stores in 2026 and
opening approximately 20 stores in 2026-2027. The company allocated $5.5
billion for fiscal 2026 remodels across its 5,200+ U.S. locations.

**Costco Growth**: The warehouse retailer targets 30+ new stores annually
and expects 28 net openings in fiscal 2026, operating nearly 640 U.S.
locations.

**Aldi's Flexible Format**: Aldi South Group is testing a modular store
design launched in Aventura, Florida (September 2025) allowing adaptation
to "smaller urban footprints" while maintaining cost discipline. The
company is opening 150 U.S. stores this year.

## Investment Activity

Grocery-anchored center transactions totaled nearly $11 billion in
2025—a 42% increase from 2024. Recent deals include:

- Brixton Capital acquired two centers for $130 million combined
- Medipower purchased a seven-property portfolio for $115 million
- Nuveen invested $298 million in a seven-property portfolio
- Blackstone acquired three largely grocery-anchored portfolios (130
  properties) for $6.7 billion (2025-2026)

## Emerging Concepts

**Small-Format Innovation**: Gelson's opened "ReCharge by Gelson's"
micromarkets (3,000-5,400 sq. ft.) pairing groceries with EV charging
hubs, lounges, and fast-casual food in Southern California.

**Restaurant Competition**: According to Tillster's 2026 survey, 36% of
consumers visit grocery stores more frequently and 33% shop c-stores more
often, while 29-37% reduce fast-food/fast-casual visits, indicating
"nontraditional foodservice players" are capturing dining occasions.

## Market Dynamics

Industry research confirms "the days when any grocery store could anchor
a center equally well are fading." Value-oriented and fresh-format
concepts now drive growth, creating distinct market stratification
between discount grocers, traditional supermarkets, and specialty
formats.
